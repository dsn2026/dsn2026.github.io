Welcome Reception

Kick off DSN 2026 at the Welcome Reception, a relaxed evening gathering where attendees can meet fellow researchers, reconnect with colleagues, and enjoy drinks and light refreshments in a relaxed atmosphere.

The reception will be held at CO-LAB, UNC Charlotte's innovation and entrepreneurship hub located at the Center City campus, directly across the street from the main conference venue at the Dubois Center.

🕔 Time: 5:30 PM - 7:30 PM
📍 Location: CO-LAB, UNC Charlotte Center City, 301 E 9th St, Charlotte, NC 28202
