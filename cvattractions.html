Below are some examples of attractions and activities visitors may enjoy while in Charlotte:

Charlotte Uptown

The vibrant heart of the city, Uptown Charlotte is home to major business districts, cultural institutions, restaurants, and entertainment venues. Visitors can explore museums, enjoy nightlife, and take in the skyline views while walking through its lively streets.

University of North Carolina at Charlotte

A major public research university known for its diverse academic programs and modern campus. The University of North Carolina at Charlotte offers scenic green spaces, state-of-the-art facilities, and hosts various cultural and sporting events throughout the year.

NASCAR Hall of Fame

An interactive museum celebrating the history of NASCAR, featuring racing simulators, historic cars, and hands-on exhibits.

U.S. National Whitewater Center

A massive outdoor adventure park offering whitewater rafting, kayaking, zip-lining, rock climbing, and scenic trails.

Carowinds

A large amusement and water park featuring thrilling roller coasters, family rides, and live entertainment.

Freedom Park

A popular urban park with walking trails, sports facilities, and a scenic lake, ideal for outdoor relaxation.

Charlotte Motor Speedway

A world-famous motorsports venue where visitors can watch races or experience high-speed driving activities.
