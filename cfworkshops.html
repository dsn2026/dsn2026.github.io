DSN-2026: Workshops

The workshops at DSN'2026 aim to provide a forum where researchers can gather and engage in discussions about various aspects of dependability-related research and its practical applications. These workshops cover a wide range of topics, serving as incubators for specific scientific communities with shared research goals. They also offer a valuable opportunity for researchers to exchange and explore scientific ideas in their early stages, well before reaching the level of maturity required for conference or journal publication.
